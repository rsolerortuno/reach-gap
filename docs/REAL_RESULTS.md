# Real results in v0.8.0

## RCC target geometry

The real RCC package contains 465,534 cells. Within pathology-defined tumour regions, VISTA had the highest target-positive fraction and the lowest primary median structural-vessel distance among the four compared targets.

| Target | Positive fraction | Median distance | Positive within 50 µm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| VISTA | 43.94% | 13.39 µm | 94.52% |
| LAG-3 | 23.09% | 16.64 µm | 93.28% |
| PD-1 | 17.61% | 16.78 µm | 94.43% |
| PD-L1 | 4.64% | 16.63 µm | 89.72% |

## Relative uncertainty analysis

Across 20,000 draws and six vessel definitions:

- VISTA rank-1 probability: 98.525%;
- PD-1 rank-1 probability: 0.895%;
- PD-L1 rank-1 probability: 0.580%;
- LAG-3 rank-1 probability: 0%.

VISTA beat PD-1 in 99.105%, PD-L1 in 99.420% and LAG-3 in 100% of draws.

## Evidence readiness

The fixed readiness audit scored 40.5/100, with 3 of 8 requirements supported by same-tissue evidence. Absolute reachability remains blocked.

## External validation

Independent evidence supports the component methods but is not silently transferred to RCC. This includes four-field perfusion-proxy validation, source-protocol HER2 calibration, literature IgG transport priors and native breast Xenium ERBB2 extraction.

## Interpretation

The relative result is scientifically useful because it is robust under the declared geometry-expression objective. It does not show that VISTA is the best therapeutic target, that an administered antibody reaches VISTA-positive cells, or that a patient would respond.
